I attended the [AI WILD](https://wildwestworkshop.github.io/) workshop on inverse problems and imaging.
I heard very good talks there, I think the one I liked the most was from [Pierre Weiss](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mH4DvxQAAAAJ&hl=fr), presenting his work with Hai on [Analytical solutions for CNN inverse problem solvers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10334)!
I like his honest views, and the fact he uses plain language.
Of the course the talk of [Julian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=u_hH-fUAAAAJ&hl=es) was also nice but I think I heard it 5 times by now, although this time it had a bit of [RAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08915).
The contributed presentation of [Alessio Spagnoletti](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Xmr-06oAAAAJ&hl=en) on [latino-pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12615) was also cool

On my side, I presented for the firs time what I've been cooking for 6 months now **MLEM as a Bregman mirror descent**.
I met [Alix Chazottes](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=25aZXVIAAAAJ&hl=fr) who told me she was working on similar stuff, her work on [variable metric Bremgan methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12829) ended up being quite different.
I also presented my work to [Antonin Clerc](https://www.researchgate.net/scientific-contributions/Antonin-Clerc-2337582813) and [Nicolas Papadakis](https://scholar.google.fr/citations?user=hfyLiLYAAAAJ&hl=en) who were actually working on exactly the same thing !
It seems they were a few months behind though, they thought the reparameterized objective was convex: been there done that !
As of late September 2026 my pre-print on this is still not out, while theirs was already out in February 🥲
To be fair, the work has evolved into something quite different in the mean time.
