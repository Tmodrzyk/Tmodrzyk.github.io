I attended the workshop **Parcimonie, Optimisation et Problèmes Inverses Lyon Saint-Etienne Savoir** 2026 in Saint-Etienne ([POPILSS 2026](https://popils-day.github.io/)).
The talks were nice, but one definitely stood out !
The designs from [Ammar Mian](https://ammarmian.fr/) were awesome.
I just checked his personal website and he has some sort of crazy interactive tower now.
He also seems to have many other resources that I believe are worth checking out: [https://github.com/AmmarMian?tab=repositories](https://github.com/AmmarMian?tab=repositories)

The talk was about semi-positive matrices and how to learn neural networks for these specific structures.
I knew a bit of [Riemannian geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_geometry) and optimization so I didn't learn much, but the visualizations were very, very, **very** nice.
It reinforced my opinion on the fact that having good supports really improves a talk.
Joseph disagreed and thought that some animations were distracting.
I could personally have fully visual explanations without formulas and that would be more than enough, walls of equations are not really my thing.
